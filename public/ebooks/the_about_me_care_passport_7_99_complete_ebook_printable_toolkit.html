Introduction • Person-Centred Continuity

Bridging the Gap Between Home, Respite and Hospital

When a person with care needs is admitted to hospital or attends a respite day centre, staff often see only the medical condition, missing the unique individual beneath. A Care Passport ensures their communication style, likes, dislikes, and medical specifics travel with them everywhere.
